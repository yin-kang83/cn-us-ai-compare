# Papers

Two working papers based on this project's data and framework:

1. **The AI investment race: Revenue, capital expenditure, and financing structures in US and Chinese listed firms (2026)** — `paper1-ai-investment-race-v0.3.pdf` (14 pp.)
   Empirical comparison of 12 US and 23 Chinese listed firms; cloud revenue vs capex; cash-flow sustainability; financing structures.

2. **Making Cross-Market Industry Comparisons Auditable: An Evidence-Graded, Falsifiable Framework** — `paper2-auditable-comparison-framework-v0.2.pdf` (18 pp.)
   Methodology paper: layered decomposition, evidence grading, claim-level provenance, falsification ledger, versioned data channel with quality gates.

Data and code: https://gitee.com/yin-kang83/cn-us-ai-compare (mirror: GitHub) · Archive DOI: https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.24353870

Working papers — not peer reviewed. Comments welcome via issues.
