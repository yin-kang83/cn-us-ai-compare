# Papers

Two working papers based on this project's data and framework:

1. **The AI investment race: Revenue, capital expenditure, and financing structures in US and Chinese listed firms** — `paper1-ai-investment-race-v1.0.pdf` (v1.0 polished, 14 pp.)
   Empirical comparison of 12 US and 23 Chinese listed firms; cloud revenue vs capex; cash-flow sustainability; financing structures.

2. **Making Cross-Market Industry Comparisons Auditable: An Evidence-Graded, Falsifiable Framework** — `paper2-auditable-comparison-framework-v1.0.pdf` (v1.0 polished, 17 pp.)
   Methodology paper: layered decomposition, evidence grading, claim-level provenance, falsification ledger, versioned data channel with quality gates.

Author: Kang Yin (Independent Researcher, ORCID 0009-0001-2723-1293).
